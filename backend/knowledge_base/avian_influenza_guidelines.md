# Avian Influenza (Bird Flu) Containment & Biohazard Guidelines

## 1. Disease Profile & Signs
Avian Influenza (H5N1 / H5N8 strains) is a highly contagious zoonotic viral disease affecting domestic poultry (chickens, ducks, turkeys, quail).

### Clinical Symptoms
- Sudden unexplained mass mortality in poultry flocks without prior warning signs.
- Severe cyanosis (blue/purple discoloration) of comb, wattles, and unfeathered skin.
- Swelling of head, eyelids, comb, and hocks.
- Profuse watery greenish-white diarrhea.
- Respiratory distress, gasping for air, sneezing, coughing, and nasal discharge.
- Drop in egg production with soft-shelled or shell-less eggs.

---

## 2. Biohazard Containment & Action Plan
1. **Strict Farm Lockdown**: Seal off farm gates immediately. No movement of birds, eggs, manure, or feed vehicles in or out.
2. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: All staff handling birds MUST wear full PPE including N95 masks, goggles, gloves, and protective suits.
3. **Culling Protocol (1 km Radius)**: If Avian Influenza is confirmed by ICAR-NIHSAD Bhopal, humane culling of all birds within 1 km radius (Infected Zone) is mandatory under National Action Plan guidelines.
4. **Disinfection Protocol**: Spray coops with 2% Sodium Hypochlorite or Formalin solution.

---

## 3. Mandatory Reporting & Public Health Warning
- Immediately alert the District Magistrate, Animal Husbandry Department, and Health Officer upon suspecting high mortality.
- Avian Influenza is ZOONOTIC and can infect humans. Any farm worker presenting with fever, cough, or conjunctivitis must report to a government hospital.
