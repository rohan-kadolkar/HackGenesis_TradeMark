# Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) Standard Operating Procedure - Karnataka Animal Husbandry Department

## 1. Overview and Disease Profile
Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) is a highly contagious viral disease affecting cloven-hoofed animals including cattle, buffaloes, sheep, goats, and pigs. 

### Clinical Symptoms
- High fever (104°F - 106°F / 40°C - 41°C).
- Vesicles (blisters) on tongue, dental pad, gums, teats, and interdigital spaces of hooves.
- Profuse salivation with ropy, stringy saliva ("drooling").
- Severe lameness, reluctance to move, and loss of appetite (anorexia).
- Sudden mortality in young calves due to myocarditis ("tiger heart").

---

## 2. Emergency Isolation & Biohazard Control
1. **Immediate Quarantine**: Isolate affected animals at least 100 meters away from healthy livestock immediately.
2. **Movement Prohibition**: Ban all animal movement within a 10 km radius ring zone of the reported infected farm.
3. **Farm Disinfection**:
   - Apply 4% Sodium Carbonate (Washing Soda) or 2% Sodium Hydroxide solution daily to sheds, troughs, and equipment.
   - Use quicklime (slaked lime) at farm entry gates and pathways.
4. **Footbaths**: Install 4% washing soda footbaths at shed entryways for all handlers.

---

## 3. Treatment Guidelines & Supportive Care
*Note: FMD is viral; no specific antiviral cure exists. Treatment is symptomatic and supportive to prevent secondary bacterial infection.*
- **Mouth Lesions**: Wash oral cavity 2-3 times daily with 1% Potassium Permanganate (KMnO4) solution or 0.5% Alum solution. Apply boro-glycerine paste.
- **Foot Lesions**: Clean interdigital wounds with 1% KMnO4 solution, dry thoroughly, and apply antiseptic fly-repellent ointment (e.g., Loraxene or copper sulphate).
- **Fever & Pain Relief**: Administer NSAIDs (Meloxicam @ 0.5 mg/kg body weight IV/IM) as prescribed by a licensed veterinarian.
- **Hydration**: Provide soft green fodder, gruel (ragi/wheat porridge), and oral electrolyte solutions continuously.

---

## 4. Government Notification & Reporting SOP
- Any suspect case of FMD MUST be reported immediately to the nearest Veterinary Assistant Surgeon (VAS) or District Deputy Director within **6 hours**.
- Ring vaccination with FMD trivalent vaccine (O, A, Asia-1) must be conducted in a **10 km radius** around the focus of infection.
