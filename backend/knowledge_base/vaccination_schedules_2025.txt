KARNATAKA ANIMAL HUSBANDRY DEPARTMENT - ANNUAL LIVESTOCK VACCINATION SCHEDULE 2025

CATTLE AND BUFFALO VACCINATION CALENDAR:
1. Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) Trivalent Vaccine:
   - Initial dose at 4 months of age.
   - Booster at 1 month after initial dose.
   - Bi-annual revaccination (every 6 months: May/June and November/December).
2. Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS) Vaccine:
   - Annual vaccination before monsoon (May/June).
3. Black Quarter (BQ) Vaccine:
   - Annual vaccination in endemic districts before monsoon.
4. Anthrax Vaccine:
   - Annual vaccination in designated endemic areas.

GOAT AND SHEEP VACCINATION CALENDAR:
1. PPR (Peste des Petits Ruminants) Sungri Strain Vaccine:
   - Vaccination at 3 months of age and above.
   - Single dose gives immunity for 3 years.
2. Enterotoxaemia (ET) Vaccine:
   - Vaccination at 3-4 months of age before monsoon (May).
   - Annual booster.
3. Goat Pox Vaccine:
   - Annual vaccination in December/January.

POULTRY VACCINATION CALENDAR:
1. Newcastle Disease (Ranikhet) RDVK / F1 Strain:
   - Day 7 (Eye drop/Nasal), Booster at Day 28 (LaSota).
2. Infectious Bursal Disease (Gumboro):
   - Day 14 (Drinking water), Booster at Day 21.

SWINE VACCINATION CALENDAR:
1. Classical Swine Fever (CSF) Cell Culture Vaccine:
   - Piglets at 2-3 months of age, Annual booster for breeding stock.
2. African Swine Fever (ASF):
   - No commercial vaccine available. Strict biosecurity and culling mandatory.
