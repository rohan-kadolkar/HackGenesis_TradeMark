# African Swine Fever (ASF) Emergency Response SOP

## 1. Disease Profile & Symptoms
African Swine Fever (ASF) is a lethal viral disease affecting domestic and wild pigs. ASF is NOT zoonotic (does not infect humans) but carries near 100% mortality in pigs.

### Clinical Signs
- Sudden high fever (105°F - 107°F / 40.5°C - 42°C).
- Erythema (redness/cyanosis) of ear tips, tail, abdomen, and legs.
- Loss of appetite, extreme lethargy, and weakness.
- Bloody diarrhea, vomiting, and nasal bleeding (epistaxis).
- Abortion in pregnant sows.
- Sudden death within 6 to 13 days of exposure.

---

## 2. Containment & Biosecurity Protocols
1. **Zero Pig Movement**: Total ban on pig transportation, slaughterhouses, and pork trade in affected zones.
2. **Disinfection**: Use 2% Sodium Hydroxide (caustic soda), Citric acid, or Virkon-S for thorough facility washing.
3. **Culling & Deep Burial**: Culling within 1 km infected zone. Carcasses must be buried 2 meters deep with a layer of quicklime.
4. **No Swill Feeding**: Absolute prohibition of feeding food waste or hotel scraps to pigs.
