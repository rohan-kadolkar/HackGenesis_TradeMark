GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA - DEPARTMENT OF ANIMAL HUSBANDRY AND VETERINARY SERVICES
LEGAL NOTIFICATION & BIOSECURITY EMERGENCY PROTOCOL 2025

1. NOTIFICATION OF SCHEDULED DISEASES
Under the Prevention and Control of Infectious and Contagious Diseases in Animals Act, the following livestock diseases are declared as Scheduled Contagious Diseases:
- Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) in Cattle/Buffalo/Goats
- Peste des Petits Ruminants (PPR) in Sheep/Goats
- Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS) in Cattle/Buffalo
- Avian Influenza (H5N1/H5N8) in Poultry
- African Swine Fever (ASF) & Classical Swine Fever in Swine

2. DUTY OF LIVESTOCK OWNERS AND VETERINARIANS
Every farmer, livestock owner, and practicing veterinarian who suspects or detects a Scheduled Disease MUST report the outbreak within 6 hours to the local Veterinary Officer, District Deputy Director, or District Magistrate.

3. EMERGENCY ISOLATION AND MOVEMENT CONTROL POWERS
- The District Deputy Director is empowered to establish a 1 km Infected Zone, 3 km Surveillance Zone, and 10 km Buffer Zone.
- Transportation of livestock, animal products, dung, and fodder outside an Infected Zone is strictly prohibited.
- Checkposts with vehicle disinfection footbaths shall be established at district boundaries during outbreak declarations.

4. BIOSECURITY MANDATES FOR FARMS
- All commercial livestock and poultry farms must maintain perimeter fencing, footbaths at shed entrances, and visitor logbooks.
- Disposal of dead animals must be via deep burial (minimum 6 feet) with lime powder coating or incineration. Disposal in rivers, lakes, or open fields is a punishable offense.
