About Karnataka Biosecurity Network

Our Mission

To build a resilient, community-driven biosecurity network across Karnataka that enables rapid disease detection, response, and prevention, protecting the livelihoods of millions of livestock farmers.

The Problem

  • Many farmers do not follow proper biosecurity protocols
  • Lack of knowledge about disease transmission pathways
  • Backyard farming practices increase outbreak risks
  • Diseases cause economic disasters for smallholders
  • No real-time monitoring or early warning systems
  • Weak veterinary infrastructure in rural areas
  • Low literacy limits access to technical information
  • No personalized guidance for individual farms

Our Solution

We have built a four-tier community network connecting:

Farmers

Veterinarians

District Heads

State Heads

Real Karnataka Data

Our platform uses authentic data from the Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Services Department, Karnataka:

  • 15 districts with real livestock census data (2024-2025)
  • Actual village and taluka-level information
  • Priority diseases: FMD, PPR, Hemorrhagic Septicemia, Avian Influenza
  • Vaccination coverage statistics by district
  • Risk zones based on historical outbreak patterns
